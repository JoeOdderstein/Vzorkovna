Feed the Dragon — Quotation Outline
 
Krakow: Feed the Dragon
 
Quotation outline 
Thank you for your detailed feedback. We're delighted to hear that you and your team are excited 
about the proposed direction for Krakow Ribs and are ready to move the project into the next phase. 
Regarding the budget estimates, we completely understand the importance of receiving individual 
cost ranges for each component. This will allow you to prioritize the elements that deliver the 
greatest impact while keeping the project aligned with your budget. We understand that the current 
proposal represents a significant investment. If the proposed scope exceeds the available budget, we 
would be happy to revisit the concept together and prepare an alternative quotation with a reduced 
scope. 
Our proposed approach is as follows: 
The largest cost variable is the dragon itself. A full-scale animatronic dragon would be prohibitively 
expensive, so we've developed a more cost-effective concept that still delivers a highly memorable 
finale. 
Instead of a full-body dragon, we propose having only a big dragon's head emerge through the 
ceiling. This significantly reduces the mechanical complexity while retaining a powerful visual impact. 
The animatronics would be limited to essential movements, opening and closing the mouth, 
animated eyes, and dramatic red illumination, supported by a high-quality surround sound system 
that brings the creature convincingly to life. Since the dragon's head is an art direction decision, we 
also suggest that Ksusha review the construction process and material choices to explore 
opportunities for a more cost-effective approach. For now, we have included a budget estimate for 
the art pieces as a placeholder, which can be refined once the final design and production approach 
have been determined. 
The dragon would be visible from both the ground floor and the first floor, appearing to sleep 
peacefully throughout most of the guest experience. This central feature would serve two key 
moments within the restaurant: the presentation of the ribs upon arrival and the climactic ending of 
the dining experience. 
For the introduction, staff members would simply press a button to trigger the sequence. The dragon 
would come to life with subtle lighting and ambient sound before speaking in a sleepy voice: 
"Feed me... feed me..." 
As guests reach the climax of the rib-eating experience, the dragon suddenly awakens. Its eyes begin 
to glow an intense red, its mouth slowly opens, and a carefully synchronized combination of lighting, 
high-output smoke, and immersive sound creates the illusion that it is breathing fire. Powerful roars 
and dramatic effects transform the restaurant into a theatrical finale, delivering a memorable 
experience while keeping production costs under control.

For the presentation plates, we have intentionally kept the design more minimalistic. They will 
feature custom 3D-printed casings, but we recommend avoiding integrated electronics. This keeps 
the plates durable, easy to maintain, and significantly more cost-effective without compromising 
their visual appeal. 
Each dining table will feature a small interactive dragon head. We have now developed a cost 
estimate for a system in which every dragon head contains its own electronics, including a built-in 
scale connected to a points system. Each dragon head would also include LED lighting, a compact 
speaker, and smoke effects. As guests progress through the experience, the points collected at the 
table would also communicate with the main dragon, creating a sense that every table contributes to 
awakening the creature. 
This interactive system provides a strong foundation for the overall experience. It creates a clear 
connection between the individual table interactions and the restaurant-wide finale, making guests 
feel that they are collectively building toward the dragon's awakening. The modular nature of the 
system also makes it scalable, allowing features to be added or refined over time while keeping the 
initial implementation flexible and budget-conscious.

5. Additional expansion options: 
LED score screens 
Please note that the current proposal does not include a live scoring and leaderboard system. One option 
we have been considering is a digital display that shows the total weight of ribs consumed at each table, 
alongside a central leaderboard that allows guests to compare their scores with other tables. This would 
introduce an additional competitive element to the dining experience, encourage interaction between 
guests, and reinforce the connection between the table experience and the final dragon activation. 
If this is something you would like to explore, we would be happy to further develop the concept and 
provide a dedicated implementation plan. 
The most cost-effective solution would be to add a small LED display at each table, allowing guests to see 
their current score in real time. This would increase the total project cost to approximately €17402 
For a more immersive experience, we could also install a large display within the restaurant that presents 
a live leaderboard, showing the scores of all tables, guest rankings, and progress towards shared goals. 
This would create a stronger sense of competition and encourage interaction throughout the restaurant. 
Including both the table displays and the central leaderboard would increase the total project cost to 
approximately €18102. 
Custom loopable soundtrack 
The sound design will be developed to complement, rather than replace, the restaurant's existing 
atmosphere. The venue's own music playlist remains the primary soundtrack, while immersive audio, 
voice, and sound effects are introduced at carefully choreographed moments to maximize their 
dramatic impact. If desired, we can also develop a custom musical score or themed soundtrack 
specifically for the dragon experience, similar to the tank shot experience. This can be included as an 
additional option in the quotation if preferred!  
We would also like to provide some context on our response time. Preparing these budgets is a detailed 
process that requires extensive research into materials, production methods, logistics, and technical 
integration. Every installation we design is custom-made and developed from the ground up, from the 
hardware and fabrication to the interfaces that bring the concept to life. Because of this, creating accurate 
budget estimates takes time, and we appreciate your patience throughout the process. 
 
Please note: As we are currently still negotiating the rights, they have not been included in this quotation.
